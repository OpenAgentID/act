# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

This project exists to build useful, high-quality work through open collaboration.
The Code of Conduct exists only to keep the project productive and prevent actual harm.
We judge contributions by merit: quality, correctness, usefulness, and alignment with project goals. Nothing else.

## Expected Behavior

- Focus on the work, not the person.
- Be clear, direct, and honest in communication.
- Give and receive technical criticism constructively.
- Assume good faith unless evidence shows otherwise.
- Disagree vigorously with ideas when necessary — that is how better solutions are found.
- Deliver on commitments or communicate early when you cannot.
- Keep discussions on-topic and relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or targeted harassment (including threats, doxxing, or repeated unwanted contact).
- Deliberate disruption (trolling, spamming, derailing technical discussions).
- Plagiarism, lying about contributions, or other dishonesty.
- Actual threats of violence or credible intent to harm participants.

**Important clarification:**
Offense at strong opinions, politically incorrect language, blunt criticism, or disagreement is **not** harassment.
We do not police tone, humor, or "microaggressions."
We do not require compelled speech (e.g. pronouns).
We do not maintain lists of protected characteristics or demand ideological conformity.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will review reported incidents promptly and fairly.
Possible consequences (depending on severity):

- Private warning
- Temporary block from issues/PRs
- Permanent ban from the project

Decisions are made by maintainers and are final. Appeals are possible only with substantial new evidence.

## Reporting

Report violations to the maintainers at <eng@l1fe.ai>.
Provide the relevant links, timestamps, and context. Anonymous reports are accepted but harder to act on.

We will protect reporters from retaliation.
